## Route-audit conclusions

- **Route availability:** 62/62 route records rendered by direct navigation. Direct reloads passed after allowing for configured mock latency.
- **Unknown route:** a non-existent path rendered the branded not-found view rather than a blank screen.
- **Permission guard:** confirmed with a Business Viewer attempting `/admin/workspace`.
- **Entitlement guard:** confirmed with a workspace lacking Automation.
- **Feature-flag guard:** failed. The AI Agents flag controls navigation visibility but `/ai/agents` has no corresponding route metadata, so direct access remains possible.
- **Landmarks:** `/login`, `/explore`, `/reports/new`, `/reports/:id`, `/ai/assistant`, `/automation/new`, `/automation/:id`, and `/settings/personal` expose a `main` inside the application `main`.
- **Loading behavior:** several list routes briefly render zero-result copy before mock requests settle instead of a dedicated loading state.
- **Titles/breadcrumbs:** tested routes displayed the expected product title and route context; `/dashboards/published` reuses the generic Dashboards page rather than providing a clearly distinct published-only experience.
